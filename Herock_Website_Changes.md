## 1. Sitewide Changes

### 1.1 Remove all social media icons
Confirmed present in three places, all linking to `#` (non-functional placeholders), appearing on every page:

- **Top utility bar**, left of the "Request a Sample" button — 5 icons.
- **Footer**, directly under the logo and tagline — 6 icons.
- **Contact page**, "Follow Us" row under Office Hours — 6 icons.

**Change:** Remove all social icon links sitewide (navbar, footer, contact page) until real, active social profiles exist to link to.

## 2. Homepage Structure Changes

### 2.1 Remove "Latest News" section
Currently shows three placeholder articles ("Herock Envirotech Wins Green Innovation Award 2024," etc.) dated 2024. None of these appear in any of the supplied materials — they read as filler/template content.

**Change:** Remove the "Latest News" section and its "View All" link from the homepage.

### 2.2 Remove "Our Trusted Partners" section
Lists six abbreviations (NNPC, CHV, TTE, NIM, UN, GEF, OND) with no logos and no corresponding mention in the brochure, the website structure doc, or the NCDMB presentation.

**Change:** Remove the "Our Trusted Partners" section from the homepage. Reinstate later only once real, confirmed partner logos are available.

## 4. Homepage Hero — Updated Copy

The current hero ("INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS. CLEANER ENVIRONMENT.") is generic and doesn't pull from Herock's own materials. The brochure's opening line is stronger, more specific, and keeps circular economy front and center — so the new hero is built from that.

**Eyebrow / badge**
> Biosorbent Oil Spill Solutions · Circular Economy Services

**Headline**
> TRANSFORMING WASTE INTO SOLUTIONS
> THAT PROTECT OUR BLUE ECONOMY.

**Subhead**
> Herock Envirotech is a Nigerian cleantech startup turning agricultural waste into biodegradable, low-cost products that restore ecosystems and create economic value — starting with our flagship Chicken Feather Oil Spill Sorbent Pillow.

**CTAs** (unchanged): Explore Solutions · Our Products · Partner With Us

This keeps the existing layout and button structure intact — only the eyebrow, headline, and subhead text change.
